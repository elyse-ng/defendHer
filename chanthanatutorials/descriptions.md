## Right-Handed Punch

* Step back with your right leg.
* Make fists and bring your right hand near your chin.
* Place your left hand in front of your face.
* Extend your right arm and punch at nose level.

## Right-Legged Kick

* Step back with your right leg.
* Raise your right knee to 90°.
* Extend your leg while pushing your hips forward.
* Retract your knee to 90°.
* Return your right leg to the starting position.

## Self-Defence Scenario 1: Haymaker Swing

* Guard your head with your left hand.
* Lunge forward and punch with your right hand.

## Self-Defence Scenario 2: Punch

* Step back with your right leg and parry the incoming punch with your right hand.
* Perform a front kick with your back leg.
* Return to the starting position.
