LEVEL 3: KICK! CREATE DISTANCE WITH YOUR OPPONENT!!

Steps For Learning to Kick
  • 1. Step back with your right leg.
  • 2. Raise your right knee to 90°.
  • 3. Extend your leg while pushing your hips forward.
  • 4. Retract your knee to 90°.
  • 5. Return your right leg to the starting position.
