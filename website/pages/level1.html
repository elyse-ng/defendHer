LEVEL 1: PUNCH! THE BEST DEFENCE IS OFFENCE!!

Steps For Learning to Punch
  • 1. Step back with your right leg.
  • 2. Make fists and bring your right hand near your chin.
  • 3. Place your left hand in front of your face.
  • 4. Extend your right arm and punch at nose level.
