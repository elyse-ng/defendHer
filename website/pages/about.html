About DEFENDHER

DefendHer is made by women, with the goal of empowering other women to learn self defense. We want all girls to know what to do in an emergency situation, and to feel safer in their daily lives.

While our main focus is female empowerment, we want everyone of all genders and backgrounds to learn from our lessons, in order to defend themselves and the ones they love.

In order to curate our lessons, we followed the expertise of teammate Chanthana, a Karate Black Belt, who also appears in the tutorial videos. Users upload videos, we analyse them and provide feedback!

So, whats the tech?
Well, our website utilises HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, to provide a smooth interface for our lessons. While our backend consists of utilisation of Google's MediaPipe Pose Estimation model to extract keypoints from videos of users practicing their moves. As well as a Long-Short-term Memory (LSTM) model to classify good moves from bad moves. We also use FastAPI to bring it all together!
